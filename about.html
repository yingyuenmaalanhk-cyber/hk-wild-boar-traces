關於此計劃About this project

「豬絲馬跡」是一個公開、唯讀的香港野豬活動資訊平台。我們把政府公開發佈的野豬捕捉行動資料， 以地圖與統計圖表呈現，讓任何人都可以方便地查閱：哪裡曾經有行動、甚麼時候、涉及多少野豬， 以及各地點的近期活動程度。

本計劃最初為參加 PolyU FCE Build a Smart City Competition 2026 而開發， 現已進一步整理為一個公開、唯讀的 GIS 資訊網站，讓公眾更容易查看相關資料及分析結果。

本網站只供瀏覽：不接受資料提交、不設帳戶、不提供任何修改資料的功能。 所有訪客看到的內容完全相同。

HK Wild Boar Traces (豬絲馬跡) is an open, read-only information platform on wild boar activity in Hong Kong. Government-published wild boar capture-action data is presented through maps and statistical charts, so that anyone can easily find out where actions took place, when, how many wild boars were involved, and how recent the activity is at each location.

This project was originally developed as an entry for the PolyU FCE Build a Smart City Competition 2026. It has since been transformed into a public, read-only GIS information website for easier access to the project's data visualization and analysis.

This website is for viewing only: it does not accept data submissions, has no accounts, and offers no way to modify any data. Every visitor sees exactly the same content.

資料來源Data sources

本站所有紀錄均整理自 漁農自然護理署「野豬滋擾」網頁 公佈的野豬捕捉行動資料（行動編號、日期、地點、野豬數目）。 網站資料會定期與官方公佈的最新資料同步更新。

需要特別說明：資料描述的是政府行動紀錄，並非市民目擊報告， 亦非全港野豬數量普查。捕捉行動通常在野豬出沒或曾構成滋擾的地點進行， 因此可以作為野豬活動分佈的參考，但不等同於實際野豬活動的全貌。

官方網頁僅逐項列出最近期的行動紀錄；更早的行動只有匯總數字 （例如：2021 年 11 月至 2026 年 6 月，共 1,220 次行動、2,450 頭野豬—— 此匯總資料無法對應到具體地點，故不會出現在本站的地圖或圖表中）。 因此本站的時間序列不連續，詳見下文「資料限制」。

地圖上顯示的位置為地點的近似位置，僅供參考，並非行動的精確地點。

All records on this website are compiled from the wild boar capture-action information (action number, date, location, number of wild boars) published on the AFCD "Wild Pig Nuisance" webpage. The website is periodically updated to match the latest officially published data.

Please note: the data describe government action records, not public sighting reports, and not a territory-wide wild boar population survey. Capture actions usually take place at locations where wild boars have been present or have caused nuisance, so the data are a useful reference for the distribution of wild boar activity, but they do not represent the full picture of actual activity.

The official webpage itemises only the most recent actions; earlier actions are published as totals only (for example: November 2021 to June 2026 — 1,220 actions and 2,450 wild boars in total; such totals cannot be mapped to specific locations and therefore do not appear on this website's map or charts). The time series on this website is therefore not continuous — see "Data limitations" below.

Locations shown on the map are approximate and for reference only; they are not the exact spots of the operations.

活動指標是怎樣理解的？How to read the activity indicator

本站的「活動程度」是一個由公開紀錄推算的活動參考指標， 只反映某地點「最近一次有行動紀錄」距離資料截止日多久：

  • 高活動 — 最近 30 天內曾有行動紀錄
  • 中度活動 — 最近 31–90 天內曾有行動紀錄
  • 低度活動 — 最近 91–180 天內曾有行動紀錄
  • 較早紀錄 — 最近一次行動已超過 180 天

每個地點同時顯示紀錄次數、野豬合計數目與最近行動日期，方便自行判斷。 這個指標不是風險預測：它不能預測野豬會否出現，亦不代表某地點 現時是否危險。如需關於野豬滋擾的官方資訊，請參考 漁護署網頁。

The "activity level" on this website is a reference indicator derived from the public records. It only reflects how long ago a location's most recent action record was, relative to the data cut-off date:

  • Higher activity — a record within the last 30 days
  • Moderate activity — a record 31–90 days ago
  • Lower activity — a record 91–180 days ago
  • Earlier records only — the last action was more than 180 days ago

Each location also shows the number of records, the total number of wild boars and the date of the most recent action, so you can judge for yourself. This indicator is not a risk forecast: it cannot predict whether wild boars will appear, and it does not indicate whether a location is currently dangerous. For official information on wild boar nuisance, please refer to the AFCD webpage.

常見問題Frequently asked questions

為什麼 2025 年 9 月至 2026 年 6 月之間沒有紀錄？Why are there no records between September 2025 and June 2026?
官方網頁只逐項公佈最近期的行動（現為 2026 年 7 月起的紀錄），更早的行動只有匯總數字，本站無法取得該時段的逐項地點資料。本站收錄的 2025 年 7–8 月紀錄，是官方網頁當時逐項公佈、由本計劃較早版本保存的資料。不同時段之間因此存在空檔。 The official webpage itemises only the most recent actions (currently from July 2026 onwards); earlier actions are published as totals only, and itemised location data for the period in between is not available. The July–August 2025 records on this website were itemised on the official webpage at the time and saved by an earlier version of this project. Gaps between periods are therefore unavoidable.
我可以提交野豬目擊報告嗎？Can I submit a wild boar sighting report?
不可以。本網站是唯讀的資訊平台，不接受任何資料提交或修改。如需報告野豬滋擾，請聯絡漁護署或經政府熱線 1823 反映。 No. This website is a read-only information platform and does not accept data submissions or edits. To report wild boar nuisance, please contact the AFCD or call the government hotline 1823.
地圖上的位置準確嗎？Are the map locations accurate?
地圖上的位置為地點的近似位置，僅供參考。官方資料本身不含精確座標，因此標記位置並非實際行動的精確地點。 Map locations are approximate and for reference only. The official data contain no precise coordinates, so markers do not represent the exact spots of the operations.
「高活動」是否代表該處很危險？Does "Higher activity" mean the place is dangerous?
不是。活動程度只描述「最近一次有政府行動紀錄」距今多久，並非風險評估或預測。有關野豬安全資訊，請參考漁護署官方指引。 No. The activity level only describes how recently a government action was recorded there — it is not a risk assessment or forecast. For wild boar safety information, please refer to the official AFCD guidance.
資料多久更新一次？How often is the data updated?
本站資料為靜態快照，由維護者不定期重新整理。每頁頂部的「資料快照」日期即為當前資料的截止日。 The data are a static snapshot, refreshed periodically by the maintainers. The "Data snapshot" date at the top of every page is the current data cut-off date.
